SLIDE 2: PROBLEM STATEMENT (Why We Built TARIPA)
Title: The Problem with Manual Sub-Meter Billing
Bullet Points:
- Prone to Human Error: Landlords manually compute sub-meter readings (Current Reading minus Previous Reading × Rate) using paper ledgers or disconnected spreadsheets.
- Lack of Billing Transparency: Tenants often dispute utility charges because they cannot see their previous vs. present meter readings or exact kWh and Cu.M tariff computations.
- Fragmented Records: Room capacities, tenant assignments, monthly meter logs, and payment statuses (Paid, Pending, Overdue) are scattered without relational integrity.
- Security & Audit Gaps: Paper receipts and shared spreadsheets have no role-based access control or audit trail.

---
SLIDE 3: PROJECT OBJECTIVES & KEY INNOVATIONS
Title: Our Solution — The TARIPA Platform
Bullet Points:
- Automated Sub-Meter Computation: Automatically pulls previous readings, computes exact consumption (kWh & Cu.M), and applies active Meralco & Maynilad tariff rates.
- Interactive 3D Building Digital Twin: Visualizes the apartment building in an isometric 3D floor plan with live Meralco power grid & Maynilad water conduit lines, 6-room floor capacity tracking, and per-room telemetry.
- Dedicated Tenant Self-Service Portal: Gives every resident an auto-provisioned account with a Digital Sub-Meter Odometer (PREV -> PRES verification), 6-month trend comparisons, and official printable receipts.
- Relational Data Integrity & Security: Enforces strict foreign-key constraints, room capacity checks, and SAST-hardened API security.

---
SLIDE 4: 3-TIER SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
Title: 3-Tier Full-Stack Architecture
Bullet Points:
- Presentation Tier (Frontend): React 19 + Vite SPA with Liquid Glass UI, Interactive SVG 3D Isometric Digital Twin, and responsive Admin & Tenant Portals.
- Application Tier (Backend API): Node.js & Express 5 RESTful API with JWT HS256 Authentication, Role-Based Access Control (Admin vs. Tenant), and Server-Side Business Logic Validation.
- Data Tier (Database): MySQL 8.0 Relational Database (`taripa_db`) using `mysql2/promise` connection pooling and 100% parameterized prepared statements.

---
SLIDE 5: RELATIONAL DATABASE SCHEMA (ERD & 3NF DESIGN)
Title: 5-Table Normalized Database Schema (`taripa_db`)
Bullet Points:
- `rooms`: Stores room number, bedspace capacity (1–6 rooms per floor), and base rent.
- `tenants`: Stores resident profile, contact number, move-in date, status, and foreign key `room_id -> rooms(id) ON DELETE SET NULL`.
- `tenant_accounts`: Stores auto-generated `@username` and BCrypt-hashed password linked `1:1` to `tenants(id) ON DELETE CASCADE`.
- `utility_rates`: Stores active tariff rates per unit for Electricity (`₱/kWh`) and Water (`₱/Cu.M`).
- `meter_readings` & `billing_records`: Tracks monthly `previous_reading`, `current_reading`, computed consumption, itemized charges, and payment status (`Pending`, `Paid`, `Overdue`).

---
SLIDE 6: CORE ADMIN WORKFLOW & AUTOMATION
Title: End-to-End Admin Management Workflow
Bullet Points:
1. Room & Capacity Control: Create rooms with strict bedspace capacity limits; system prevents over-assigning tenants to full rooms.
2. Zero-Touch Tenant Provisioning: Registering a tenant automatically creates their portal login (`@firstname.lastname`) and default hashed password.
3. Smart Meter Reading Entry: Selecting a tenant auto-fills their previous reading and prevents meter rollback (`Current < Previous` is blocked).
4. One-Click Invoice Generation: Multiplies consumption by current Meralco/Maynilad rates, tracks collections via a 12-Month Trend Bar Graph, and issues official statements.

SLIDE 7: FEATURE SPOTLIGHT — 3D BUILDING DIGITAL TWIN
Title: Interactive 3D Isometric Building Digital Twin
Bullet Points:
- Real-Time Architectural Visualization: Converts standard room cards into an interactive 3D isometric campus view with 1st Floor (6/6 capacity) and 2nd Floor switching.
- Meralco & Maynilad Grid Simulation: Displays animated power lines and water pipelines flowing from the Main Grid Hub (with Meralco & Maynilad service trucks) directly into each occupied room's sub-meter.
- Live Unit Telemetry Inspector: Clicking any 3D room unit opens a glass HUD showing bedspace occupancy slots, real-time kWh & Cu.M usage, and unpaid bill alerts.

SLIDE 8: FEATURE SPOTLIGHT — TENANT SELF-SERVICE PORTAL
Title: Transparent Tenant Portal & Sub-Meter Odometer
Bullet Points:
- Digital Sub-Meter Odometer Card: Allows tenants to verify their exact `PREV -> PRES` meter readings for both Electricity (kWh) and Water (Cu.M) with a live `Synced` indicator.
- Itemized Bill Breakdown & Gauges: Shows circular consumption gauges and a 6-month historical comparison (`% vs. 6-month average`).
- Instant Payment & Official Receipt: Supports GCash, Maya, and Bank Transfer settlement with an instant printable Official Receipt (`OR-XXXX`).

---
SLIDE 9: SYSTEM SECURITY & DEFENSIVE HARDENING (SAST AUDIT)
Title: Enterprise-Grade Security & Data Privacy
Bullet Points:
- 100% SQL Injection Immunity: All database queries across all 7 route modules use parameterized prepared statements (`?`).
- Strict RBAC & IDOR Protection: Separate `adminAuth` and `clientAuth` middlewares with pinned `HS256` JWT verification; tenants can only query their own `req.clientTenantId`.
- Brute-Force & DoS Protection: 5-attempt login rate limiter with a 30-second lockout (`HTTP 429`), JSON type/length guards, and a `100kb` payload limit.
- HTTP Security Headers & XSS Sanitization: Protected with `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, and HTML escaping on printable billing statements.
